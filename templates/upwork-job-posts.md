# Upwork Job Post Templates

## Script Writer

**Title:** YouTube Script Writer for Faceless Channel — Ongoing Work

**Description:**
```
I run a faceless YouTube channel and need a reliable script writer
for ongoing video production.

What you'll receive:
- A detailed video outline with segment breakdowns, talking points,
  and evidence/claims
- A Script Blueprint document that defines the exact voice, tone,
  pacing, and structure to follow

What I need:
- A full script (1,500–2,500 words depending on video length)
  written in the exact voice and style defined in the Script Blueprint
- Scripts delivered within 48 hours of receiving the outline
- Willingness to follow the blueprint precisely — this is not
  creative writing, it's execution from a proven formula

Budget: $15–25 per script
Volume: 4–8 scripts per month
```

## Video Editor

**Title:** YouTube Video Editor — Faceless Channel (Stock Footage + Voiceover)

**Description:**
```
I need a video editor for a faceless YouTube channel. You'll be
working with AI voiceover and stock footage — no camera footage.

What you'll receive:
- A completed script with B-roll/visual direction notes
- AI-generated voiceover audio file
- Brand guidelines (fonts, colors, lower thirds style)

What I need:
- Edited video (8–15 minutes) with stock footage, transitions,
  text overlays, and background music
- Engaging visual pacing that matches the script's energy
- Delivery within 72 hours
- Revisions as needed (typically 1 round)

Software: Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or Final Cut Pro
Budget: $35–45 per video
Volume: 4–8 videos per month
```

## Thumbnail Designer

**Title:** YouTube Thumbnail Designer — High-CTR Thumbnails

**Description:**
```
I need a thumbnail designer who can create high-CTR thumbnails
for a faceless YouTube channel.

What you'll receive:
- The video title
- Key visual concept or subject
- Reference thumbnails from competitors

What I need:
- 1 thumbnail per video (1280x720)
- Bold, clean design with high contrast
- Text overlay if applicable
- 2 variations to A/B test
- Delivery within 24 hours

Software: Photoshop or Figma
Budget: $5–10 per thumbnail
Volume: 4–8 thumbnails per month
```
