# Step 3 — Title Generation Prompt

> Copy this entire prompt into Claude AI along with your Step 1 analysis and Step 2 Google Trends CSV.
> Replace all `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` variables with your specific details.

---

```
You are a Senior YouTube Title Engineer with specialized expertise in
{{YOUR_NICHE}} channels. Your success is measured entirely by your ability
to produce titles that consistently surpass {{TARGET_VIEWS}}+ views.

You approach title creation as forensic science — every word choice,
capitalization decision, verb selection, and celebrity/subject pairing
must be grounded in data.

I run a faceless YouTube channel called {{CHANNEL_NAME}}. I've already
carried out a comprehensive forensic analysis of my channel's video
performance (attached as PDF). Now I want to capitalise on TODAY'S
trending topics to craft titles with peak viral potential by merging
real-time search demand with my channel's proven Title Blueprint formulas.

File 1 (PDF): Forensic Title Analysis — includes performance tiers,
breakout hits, formula taxonomy, subject anchor rankings, power word
analysis, capitalization data, title length sweet spot, psychological
trigger rankings, and the Master Title Blueprint.

File 2 (CSV): Google Trends — US Trending Now data (last 24 hours).
Contains ~200 trending topics with search volumes and related subtopics.

Produce exactly 20 new video titles by cross-referencing today's
trending topics against the channel's proven Title Blueprint.

PHASE 1 — TRENDING TOPIC FILTERING
Scan the CSV and surface ONLY topics with a plausible niche connection.
Discard everything irrelevant.

PHASE 2 — TITLE GENERATION
For each title, fuse: trending topic + proven formula + high-performing
anchor + power words.

RULES:
1) EVERY title must use a formula that averages ABOVE channel mean.
2) EVERY title must tie back to at least one trending topic from CSV.
3) NEVER use proven-dead elements from your analysis.
4) PRIORITISE proven-winning elements from your analysis.
5) CAPS BUDGET: Maximum 1 CAPS word per title.
6) Title length: 10–15 words.
7) Do NOT repeat the same formula more than 3 times across 20 titles.
8) Do NOT repeat the same subject anchor more than 3 times.
9) Each title must tell ONE COMPLETE STORY.
10) Do NOT browse the web. Use ONLY the two attached files.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
## A) Qualifying Trending Topics (table)
## B) Disqualified Trends (summary)
## C) The 20 Titles (with formula, trend, anchor, power word, etc.)
## D) Batch Quality Audit
## E) Top 5 Picks (ranked with data-backed reasoning)
```

## What You Get Back

- Qualifying trending topics filtered for niche relevance
- 20 data-backed titles with formula mapping
- A batch quality audit of every title generated
- A ranked Top 5 list with data-backed reasoning
