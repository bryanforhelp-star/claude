# Fruit Drama — Title & Caption Generation Prompt

> Use this to generate titles, video descriptions, on-screen text, and series naming.

---

## Part A — Video Title Generation

```
You are a TikTok/Shorts title engineer for an AI-generated fruit drama channel.

The channel posts 10–30 second videos of anthropomorphic fruit characters
in emotional drama storylines: betrayal, revenge, conflict, absurd twists.

Target audience: American TikTok and YouTube Shorts users aged 13–28.
Tone: absurd, chaotic, dramatic, fast-paced.

Generate 10 video titles for each of the following episode types:

1. BETRAYAL episode — banana discovers cheating
2. REVENGE episode — broccoli gets back at strawberry
3. CONFLICT episode — apple confronts banana
4. ABSURD TWIST episode — tomato reveals a secret
5. CLIFFHANGER episode — ending that demands Part 2

Rules:
- American slang only
- No British slang
- Max 8 words per title
- Must imply drama or a reveal
- Must make the viewer feel they CANNOT skip this video
- Use "POV:", "…", and ellipsis strategically for tension
- Do NOT use emojis in the title itself (they belong in the description)

Output format: numbered list, 10 titles per episode type.
Flag the top 3 across all 50 with [BEST] and explain why in one sentence each.
```

---

## Part B — On-Screen Text (Aggressive Captions)

```
Generate aggressive TikTok-style on-screen caption text for this scene:

Scene description: {{PASTE SCENE FROM STORY SCRIPT}}

Rules:
- ALL CAPS for high-emotion moments
- Mixed case for setup lines
- Max 4 words per caption
- Each caption syncs to one dialogue line
- Flag which words should get the SHAKE or ZOOM effect in CapCut
- Flag where to insert a 1-second pause for dramatic effect

Format:
[LINE 1]: "CAPTION TEXT" — [effect: shake on WORD]
[LINE 2]: "caption text" — [effect: none]
[PAUSE]: 1 second
etc.
```

## Part C — Series Naming & Branding

```
Generate 10 channel/series name options for an AI-generated fruit drama TikTok channel.

Style: absurd, slightly ironic, memorable, American
Avoid: British humor, food puns that are too clever, anything that sounds like a cooking channel

The name should imply:
- Drama
- Fruit characters
- Serialized storylines ("episodes", "Part 2", etc.)

Also generate:
- 3 bio options (under 80 characters each)
- 3 pinned-post hook ideas for the first video

Output as three labeled sections.
```

## Part D — Hashtag Strategy

```
Generate a hashtag pack for an AI-generated fruit drama TikTok/Shorts video.

Video topic: {{PASTE ONE-LINE EPISODE SUMMARY}}
Platform: TikTok + YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels

Requirements:
- 5 broad hashtags (1M+ videos)
- 5 medium hashtags (100K–1M videos)
- 5 niche hashtags (under 100K — ownable)
- 2 trending hashtags (check what's spiking this week in entertainment)

Output as one flat list — ready to paste into TikTok description.
Note which 3 are highest priority based on the video topic.
```
