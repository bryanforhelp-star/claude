# Fruit Drama — Video Generation & Editing Prompts

> Use these prompts in Kling AI 3.0 (or equivalent) to animate your character images.
> Each prompt targets a specific emotional beat from the story script.

---

## Kling AI Base Prompt Formula

```
Animated [CHARACTER(S)] [ACTION], [EMOTIONAL TONE], [CAMERA MOVEMENT].
Style: American slang dialogue, emotional reactions, dramatic pacing, TikTok humor.
Mood: chaotic, overstimulating, absurd.
[SPECIFIC DIALOGUE OR SOUND CUE]
```

---

## Animation Prompt Library

### Crying / Devastation Scene

```
Animate a 3D Pixar-style banana character standing in the rain,
single tear rolling down cheek, head slowly bowing in defeat.
Camera: static shot with slow zoom in on face.
Mood: heartbroken, dramatic, overstimulating.
Style: TikTok drama format.
Duration: 8–10 seconds.
```

### Betrayal Reveal Scene

```
Animate a 3D Pixar-style banana character turning around slowly
to face the camera with a shocked expression, sunglasses falling off.
Action: banana freezes mid-motion, jaw drops, punch bowl tips over in background.
Camera: static with subtle slow zoom in.
Mood: chaotic betrayal, dramatic pause.
Duration: 8 seconds.
```

### Accusation / Confrontation Scene

```
Animate a chaotic animated argument between a banana character and a strawberry character.
Banana points finger aggressively. Strawberry raises hands defensively.
Both talking at the same time, overlapping.
Camera: static two-shot.
Style: American slang dialogue, emotional, fast pacing.
Mood: chaotic, high-energy, absurd.
Duration: 10 seconds.
```

### Villain Smirk / Power Move

```
Animate a 3D Pixar-style apple character turning slowly to face camera
with a cold, superior expression. Slow dramatic head turn.
Camera: slow push in on face — stops at tight close-up.
Mood: cold, calculated, intimidating.
Duration: 6–8 seconds.
```

### Comic Relief Reaction

```
Animate a 3D Pixar-style broccoli character sweating heavily,
eyes darting left and right nervously, hands up defensively.
Character stumbles backward. Small comedic slip.
Camera: static shot, slight shake on impact.
Mood: panicked, chaotic, comedic.
Duration: 6 seconds.
```

### Cliffhanger Ending Freeze Frame

```
Animate a 3D Pixar-style banana character staring directly into the camera,
one tear on cheek, completely frozen for 2 seconds before cut.
Camera: slow push in, stops at eyes.
Mood: devastating, unresolved, leaves viewer wanting Part 2.
Duration: 4 seconds.
```

## Multi-Clip Assembly

Each video is assembled from 2–4 clips. Standard structure:

| Clip | Duration | Purpose | Prompt Type |
|------|----------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | 2–3s | Hook — immediate emotional punch | Betrayal Reveal or Accusation |
| 2 | 3–4s | Escalation | Confrontation or Crying |
| 3 | 2–3s | Peak chaos or punchline | Comic Relief or Villain Smirk |
| 4 | 2s | Cliffhanger freeze | Cliffhanger Ending |

Total: 10–12 seconds.

## CapCut Editing Settings

### Caption Style (Aggressive Retention Format)

- Font: Bold sans-serif (Heavy or Black weight)
- Color: White text, yellow highlight on key words
- Size: Large — fills 70% of screen width
- Effects: Shake + zoom on emotional words
- Timing: One caption per dialogue line — tight sync to audio
- Background: None — text floats on video

### Retention Cuts

- Cut on every new dialogue line — no held shots unless intentional pause
- Add a 2-frame flash cut (white or black) before the cliffhanger ending
- Use speed ramp: slow to 0.8x on emotional peak, then snap back to 1x

### Sound Effects Library (CapCut built-in)

| Moment | Sound Effect |
|--------|-------------|
| Shock/reveal | "Dramatic sting" or "Oh no" bass drop |
| Crying scene | Sad piano single note |
| Confrontation | "Tension build" loop |
| Cliffhanger end | Sudden silence cut or record scratch |
| Comic moment | Bruh sound or muted trombone |

### Trending Audio Strategy

- Check TikTok Trending Sounds weekly — swap in trending audio under the scene
- Keep dialogue audible at 80% volume, background track at 20–30%
- Sad piano or emotional pop hit overlaid on crying scenes = highest retention

## Voiceover (Optional but High-Performing)

AI voice acting adds significant retention. Recommended tools:

- **ElevenLabs** — generate emotional voices per character (assign a unique voice to each fruit)
- **Character.AI Voice** — good for absurd/over-the-top deliveries
- Keep line delivery fast — TikTok viewers skip slow talkers

Character voice profiles:
- Barry (Banana): Mid-range male, slightly nasally, prone to dramatic pauses
- Shantelle (Strawberry): Higher female voice, defensive and breathy
- Brad (Broccoli): Deeper male, nervous stutter, trails off at the end of lines
- Apple rival: Cold, flat delivery — minimal emotion
