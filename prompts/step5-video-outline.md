# Step 5 — Video Outline Prompt

> Copy this entire prompt into Claude AI with your Script Blueprint attached.
> Replace `[INSERT YOUR VIDEO TITLE HERE]` with your actual chosen title from Step 3.

---

```
I want to produce a video based on this title:

[INSERT YOUR VIDEO TITLE HERE]

Research the topic thoroughly, then build a COMPLETE video outline that
gives my script writer and video editor everything they need to create
the strongest possible video.

Use the attached Script Bible as the governing reference for:

- Script structure and beat sheet
- Voice and tone guidelines
- Hook style and psychological triggers
- Transition types between segments
- Engagement mechanics (CTA placement, retention loops)
- Sentence-level style and pacing rules

The outline must include:

- Hook concept (first 30–60 seconds)
- Full segment breakdown with talking points
- Key evidence/claims per segment
- Suggested transitions between segments
- CTA placement recommendations
- Estimated runtime per segment
- B-roll / visual direction notes for the editor
```

## What You Get Back

A production-ready video outline that your script writer and video editor can execute without any additional briefing. The outline covers:

- Hook concept and psychological trigger
- Complete segment-by-segment breakdown
- Talking points and evidence for each section
- Transition suggestions between segments
- CTA placement strategy
- Runtime estimates per segment
- B-roll and visual direction notes
