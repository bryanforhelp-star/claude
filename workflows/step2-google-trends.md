# Step 2 — Research Trending Topics

**Tool:** Google Trends

## What This Step Does

Once you have the full channel analysis from Step 1, you switch over to Google Trends to pull real-time trending data. You navigate to the "Trending Now" section, set the region to the United States and the time frame to the past 24 hours, then export all of the trending topic data as a CSV file. This gives you a snapshot of what people are actively searching for right now — which you'll combine with the channel analysis in Step 3.

The power of this step is in the timing. You're not guessing what might be interesting — you're pulling live data on what millions of people are already curious about at this exact moment.

## How To Use It

### 1. Go to Google Trends
Open [trends.google.com](https://trends.google.com) in your browser.

### 2. Switch to Trending Now
Navigate to the Trending Now section instead of the default Explore view.

### 3. Set Filters
- **Region:** United States
- **Time frame:** Past 24 hours

### 4. Export as CSV
Download the full trending data as a CSV file. This typically contains ~200 trending topics with search volumes and related subtopics. You'll feed this into Claude in Step 3.

## Why This Matters

Trending topics give your titles a built-in audience. By combining proven title formulas with topics people are already searching for, you dramatically increase the odds of going viral before you even publish the video.
