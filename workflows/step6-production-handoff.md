# Step 6 — Hand Off to Production

**Tool:** Upwork.com

## What This Step Does

Once you have your video outline, the production pipeline is simple and fully delegatable. You don't film. You don't edit. You manage. The entire operation runs through Upwork freelancers at approximately $75 per video — covering scripting, voiceover, editing, and thumbnail design. This is where the system becomes a genuine business rather than a creative project. Your role shifts entirely to strategy and quality control, which you can execute comfortably in under 8 hours per week.

## The Production Pipeline

### 1. Load Titles Into Your Project Board
Move titles into Trello or your preferred project management tool. Each card should include the title, outline, and urgency tier so your team always knows what's coming next.

### 2. Hire Your Team on Upwork
Source a script writer, video editor, and thumbnail designer. At ~$75 per video, that covers end-to-end production from first draft to publish-ready file.

**Typical team breakdown:**
- Script writer: ~$15–25/video
- Video editor (with stock footage + voiceover): ~$35–45/video
- Thumbnail designer: ~$5–10/video

### 3. Delegate Each Video
Assign titles and outlines to your team. The outline gives everyone what they need — no lengthy briefs, no creative calls, no back-and-forth required.

### 4. Review and Publish
Quick quality check, upload to YouTube with the optimised title, and let the algorithm work. Consistency of publishing is the most important variable at this stage.

### 5. Repeat Monthly
Run the full workflow monthly for fresh data-driven titles. Scale by launching additional channels in new niches once the first channel is running smoothly.

## The Math

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Cost per video | ~$75 |
