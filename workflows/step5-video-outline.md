# Step 5 — Generate a Full Video Outline

**Tool:** Claude AI

## What This Step Does

With your Script Blueprint in hand, you now pick a video title and ask Claude to produce a complete video outline. Claude uses the blueprint as the governing reference for structure, voice, hooks, transitions, engagement mechanics, and pacing.

The result is a detailed outline that gives your script writer and video editor everything they need to produce the strongest possible video — without any back-and-forth. This is where the system truly shines: instead of spending hours briefing freelancers and managing creative direction, you produce a single comprehensive document that eliminates ambiguity and drives consistency across every video your team produces.

## How To Use It

### 1. Choose Your Title
Pick a title from the list Claude generated in Step 3. Prioritise the top-ranked titles from the batch quality audit.

### 2. Send the Outline Prompt
Copy the prompt from [`prompts/step5-video-outline.md`](../prompts/step5-video-outline.md), insert your chosen title, and attach the Script Blueprint from Step 4.

### 3. Hand Off to Your Team
Send the completed outline directly to your Upwork script writer and video editor. No additional briefing required.
