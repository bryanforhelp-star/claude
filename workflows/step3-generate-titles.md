# Step 3 — Generate Viral Video Titles

**Tool:** Claude AI

## What This Step Does

You go back to Claude and combine two powerful inputs: the forensic channel analysis from Step 1 and the real-time Google Trends CSV from Step 2. Using a detailed prompt, Claude cross-references today's trending topics against the channel's proven title formulas to produce 20 new video titles.

Every title is data-backed — tied to both a trending topic and a pattern that has already been proven to drive views. This isn't creative guesswork; it's systematic title engineering grounded in real performance data on one side and live search demand on the other.

## How To Use It

### 1. Return to Claude
Go back to your Claude conversation that contains the channel analysis output from Step 1.

### 2. Upload Both Files
- **File 1:** The Step 1 forensic analysis (PDF)
- **File 2:** The Google Trends CSV from Step 2

### 3. Send the Title Generation Prompt
Copy the prompt from [`prompts/step3-title-generation.md`](../prompts/step3-title-generation.md) and send it.

## What You Get Back

- Qualifying trending topics filtered for niche relevance
- 20 data-backed titles with formula mapping
- A batch quality audit of every title generated
- A ranked Top 5 list with data-backed reasoning
